1,000 POTENTIAL MORTGAGE LOAN UNDERWRITING HICCUPS
United States | 50 categories | 20 issues per category | 1,000 total issues

PURPOSE
A categorized idea/reference list of situations that may trigger documentation
requests, re-underwriting, pricing changes, delays, additional review, or denial.
These are potential issues, not automatic denials or a universal rulebook.
Applicability varies by lender, investor, loan type, property, location, and
current agency guidance. Always verify the requirements of the chosen program.
